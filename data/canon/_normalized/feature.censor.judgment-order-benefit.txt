name: Judgment Order Benefit
p: The first time on a turn that you use your Judgment ability to judge a creature, you gain the following benefit based on your order:
def: Exorcist :: You can teleport up to a number of squares equal to twice your Presence score. This movement must take you closer to the judged creature. You do not need line of effect to your destination.
def: Oracle :: You can deal holy damage equal to twice your Presence score to the judged creature.
def: Paragon :: You can vertical pull the judged creature up to a number of squares equal to twice your Presence score.
